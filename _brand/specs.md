# DIG Brand Spec, LOCKED

**Effective:** May 11, 2026
**Status:** Locked direction for both BLADE Holdings LLC and Dadzie Insurance Group (DIG)

---

## Visual Identity

### BLADE Holdings LLC (Parent)
**Variant:** G2 Festive (P1 palette + pink glitter overlay)
**File:** `blade-logo-LOCKED-G2-festive-2026-05-11.png`

Construction:
- BH monogram in cobalt (Outfit Bold)
- Burnt orange accent bar through lower portion of monogram
- Pink secondary stripe beneath the orange bar
- Pink glitter cloud overlay (~250 particles + 6 hero 4-point sparkles)
- Wordmark BLADE HOLDINGS in Work Sans Bold, deep charcoal
- Subline LIMITED LIABILITY COMPANY in pink (Instrument Sans)

### Dadzie Insurance Group (Operating Brand)
**Variant:** P1 Primary (clean wordmark, no glitter)
**File:** `dig-logo-primary-P1-LOCKED-2026-05-11.png`

Construction:
- DIG monogram in cobalt (Outfit Bold)
- Burnt orange accent bar through lower portion of monogram
- Wordmark DADZIE INSURANCE GROUP in Work Sans Bold, deep charcoal
- Subline ESTABLISHED 2026 in Instrument Sans

## Use-Case Mapping

### BLADE Holdings (G2 Festive variant)
The pink-glitter variant is the official BLADE identity. Use it on:
- Legal documents and contracts (Heathos LOC, vendor agreements, employment)
- Banking documents
- Investor decks and capital-raise materials
- Corporate filings
- Internal memos and board materials

### Dadzie Insurance Group (P1 Primary)
The clean P1 Primary is the official DIG identity. Use it on:
- Customer-facing communications
- Recruiting and agent-facing materials
- Office signage
- Business cards
- Website
- Email signatures
- Merch (polos, tumblers, badges)
- Marketing and social media

---

## Co-presentation Rules

When both logos appear on the same surface (e.g., letterhead, business card, dual-entity document):
- Use DIG-forward layout by default: DIG larger or in the dominant position, BLADE smaller in the footer area
- Use BLADE-forward layout only for legal/contract documents where the legal entity must lead
